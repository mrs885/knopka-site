# Cloud-plate generation prompts

Built-in image edit mode used the selected sun variant only to derive three
cloud-only atmosphere plates. The production logo was not sent through image
generation; it is composited later by `compose.sh`.

Shared constraints:

- remove the golden sun and every ray;
- add substantially more premium softly sculpted 3D clouds;
- leave a central opening for a later exact logo composite;
- preserve the cream background and all peach, beige, gray and blue ribbons;
- no button, icon, logo, letters, text or watermark.

## Plate 1

Broad rich cloud bank across the middle-lower area, with large rounded towers
at left and right and a centered opening. Clouds can overlap the lower edge of
the future button. Warm luminous white clouds with subtle cool shadows.

## Plate 2

Two tall luminous cloud masses act as curtains around a centered clearing,
plus a thick foreground bank. The lower third of the future button should be
hidden by the foreground clouds. Airy, abundant and warmly illuminated.

## Plate 3

Layered clouds sweep diagonally from lower left to upper right. A dense
foreground shelf and smaller lit clusters leave an oval opening just right of
center. Warm edge light and blue-gray shadow relate to the right-side ribbons.
