# Generation prompts

Built-in image generation edit mode was used with
`../tg-notification-info.png` as the immutable edit target.

Shared constraints for all variants:

- square Telegram channel notification artwork;
- replace only the central black information button;
- preserve the original cream background and every peach, beige, gray and
  blue translucent ribbon shape;
- preserve framing, perspective and empty margins;
- no text, letters, logo, watermark or extra objects;
- do not crop, repaint or move the background.

## Variant 1

Replace the button with a large warm golden 3D sun in the same central position
and approximate visual footprint. Hide the lower-left third behind one rounded
soft white cloud. Add a few clean dimensional rays, a subtle warm glow and a
realistic soft ground shadow. Use polished tactile 3D materials and bright
morning light.

## Variant 2

Replace the button with a vivid golden sun slightly higher and farther back,
emerging above a low horizontal soft cloud bank. Use longer elegant tapered
rays in a full halo, with the cloud covering only the lower quarter. Make the
silhouette airy and energetic, with sculptural 3D depth and radiant warm light.

## Variant 3

Replace the button with a sleek luminous amber sun sphere slightly upper-right
of the original center. Sweep one sculptural pale cloud diagonally across its
lower-left edge. Use a restrained set of short broad rounded rays, strong warm
rim light, a glossy sun and a matte cloud with subtle silver-blue shadow.
